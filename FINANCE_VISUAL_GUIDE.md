## 🚀 Quick Start (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Access Dashboard
1. Open your browser
2. Go to your app URL
3. Navigate to **Finances** in the sidebar
4. Or go directly to `/finances`

### Step 2: Review Your Metrics
1. Look at the **4 KPI cards** at the top
2. Check **Profit Margin %** - aim for 20%+ (green)
3. Review **Net Profit/Loss** - track if it's positive

### Step 3: Analyze Trends
1. Click different **Period buttons** to see different timeframes
2. Watch **Revenue vs Expenses Chart** - look for uptrends
3. Review **Breakdowns** to identify best products and high costs
4. Check **Recent Transactions** for latest activity

## 📊 Understanding Each Section

### Section 1: KPI Cards
```
┌──────────────────┐
│ 💰 METRIC        │
│  VALUE           │
│ Supporting text  │
└──────────────────┘
```
**What it shows**: One key metric with visual context
**How to read**: 
- Card color = status (Green=good, Red=bad, Amber=warning)
- Number = the actual metric value
- Text = what the number means
- Arrow = trending up/down compared to previous period

### Section 2: Revenue vs Expenses Chart
```
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ 📈 Chart Title          │

│ 🟩 Green = Revenue      │
```

**What it shows**: Daily revenue and expense trends
**How to read**:
- Taller green bars = more revenue that day
- Taller red bars = more expenses that day

## 🔍 What Each Metric Means

### Total Revenue
- **Definition**: Money coming in from sales
- **Includes**: Rabbit sales, by-product sales (manure, urine, pelts)
- **Goal**: Higher is better (more sales)
- **Action**: Increase by selling more/selling higher prices

### Total Expenses
- **Definition**: Money going out for operations
- **Includes**: Feed, Medical, Equipment, Labor, Utilities, etc.
- **Goal**: Lower is better (less cost) but don't cut quality
- **Action**: Reduce by finding better suppliers, saving resources

### Net Profit/Loss
- **Definition**: Revenue minus Expenses
- **Formula**: $Revenue - $Expenses = $Profit
- **Goal**: Positive number (profitable farm)
- **Action**: Increase revenue AND decrease expenses

### Profit Margin %
- **Definition**: How much profit from each dollar of revenue
- **Formula**: ($Profit ÷ $Revenue) × 100
- **Goal**: 20%+ is excellent, 10-20% is good, <10% is tight
- **Action**: Improve by getting more revenue or cutting costs

## 📈 Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: High Revenue, High Profit ✅
```
Revenue: $5,000  Expenses: $2,000  Profit: $3,000 (60%)
```
**Status**: Excellent! You're running efficiently
**Action**: Maintain current practices, maybe expand

### Scenario 2: High Revenue, Low Profit ⚠️
```
Revenue: $5,000  Expenses: $4,500  Profit: $500 (10%)
```
**Status**: Selling well but costs are too high
**Action**: Reduce expenses (feed, medical, equipment, labor)

### Scenario 3: Low Revenue, High Expenses ❌
```
Revenue: $2,000  Expenses: $3,000  Profit: -$1,000 (-50%)
```
**Status**: Losing money - urgent action needed
**Action**: Increase sales AND cut non-essential costs

### Scenario 4: Low Revenue, Low Profit ❌
```
Revenue: $2,000  Expenses: $1,500  Profit: $500 (25%)
```
**Status**: Struggling with sales volume
**Action**: Increase revenue through better marketing/pricing

## 💡 Tips for Using the Dashboard

### 1. Check Weekly
Make it a habit every Monday morning:
- Review last week's KPIs
- Look for any concerning trends
- Plan actions if needed

### 2. Compare Periods
Use different period buttons:
- Compare this month to last month
- Look for seasonal patterns
- Track improvements

### 3. Focus on Profit Margin
- This is your key metric
- 20%+ means healthy farm
- Less than 10% means you're breaking even

### 4. Look for Patterns
- Which products sell best?
- Which expenses are highest?
- What time of year is busiest?

### 5. Take Action
If you see problems:
- High expenses? Negotiate with suppliers
- Low sales? Improve marketing
- Imbalanced products? Focus on best sellers

## 🚨 Key Warning Signs

Watch for these:
- ❌ Profit margin under 5% → Farm barely profitable
- ❌ Expenses growing faster than revenue → Unsustainable
- ❌ Red money bar larger than green → Losing money
- ❌ No revenue some days → Sales problem
- ❌ One category > 50% of expenses → Concentration risk

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "No Data Displayed"
**Solutions**:
1. Check if you have sales in the database
2. Click different period buttons
3. Refresh the page (F5)
4. Check browser console (F12) for errors

### Problem: "Chart Looks Empty"
**Solutions**:
1. Make sure you have transactions in the selected period
2. Try "All Time" period to see if data exists
3. Check if dates are correct in your transactions

### Problem: "Numbers Look Wrong"
**Solutions**:
1. Verify currency (all amounts in USD)
2. Check date range selected
3. Confirm transaction amounts in database

### Problem: "Page Loads Slowly"
**Solutions**:
1. Be patient - first load takes 2-3 seconds
2. Subsequent period changes are faster
3. Close other browser tabs

### Problem: "Can't Navigate Here"
**Solutions**:
1. Click "Finances" in sidebar
2. Or go directly to `/finances` in address bar
3. Make sure you're logged in to the app
